Two findings stand out with solid n (≥90 each side):
- **Momentum is inverted**: high-momentum-scored coins double *less* often (11% vs 18%) and hit +50% less often (14% vs 26%) than low-momentum ones. Classic "buying the local top" pattern.

This is a plausible explanation for why the 80+ score bucket underperforms 70–79 — the top bucket is likely stacked with coins that scored well on momentum/holders for the wrong reasons.

## 7. Changes this week

Two changes, both backed by n≥90-per-side signal from the score-component breakdown, both small steps within range, both directly testable next week:

1. **weights.momentum 1.0 → 0.7** — high-momentum-scored candidates underperformed low-momentum ones (doubled 11% vs 18%, up50 14% vs 26%, n=90/184). Hypothesis: the momentum component is currently rewarding coins that have already spiked (buying near a local top). Lowering its weight should shift picks toward less-extended entries. **Confirms if**: next week's picks show higher median 24h / up50 rate and momentum's high/low split narrows or flips.

2. **weights.holders 1.0 → 0.7** — high-holders-scored candidates rugged far more than low (90% vs 49%, n=124/150). Hypothesis: this component is picking up inflated/bot-driven holder counts that precede rugs rather than genuine distribution. **Confirms if**: rug_pct among picks/passes drops and the holders high/low rug gap narrows next week.

Holding steady on min_score, stop_loss_pct, and all safety-rule thresholds — none showed a strong enough loosening case, and the exit config is already outperforming a naive hold by a wide margin, so no reason to touch it yet.

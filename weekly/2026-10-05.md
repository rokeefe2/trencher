## 4. Scoring
- **Buckets are not monotonic.** 70–79 is best (rug 61%, median 0.11). 80+ is worse (rug 79%, median 0.04). Below 70 the buckets are also poor. Doubled rates are flat at 14–15% from 60 up, so score adds almost nothing.
- **Holders** is the only component with a strong split, and it is inverted. High holders: rug 90%, median 0.01. Low holders: rug 58%, median 0.14. It is full-marks for 41% of coins. The weight is already cut to 0.75, and 14 picks since the cut (9-30) are still net negative, so it's too early to judge.
- **Liquidity:** high scores beat low ones (rug 64% vs 86%). This is the one sensible component. 67% of coins get full marks.
- **Buyer_breadth:** 91% of coins get full marks, so it can't discriminate. That is a threshold problem. High vs low (73% vs 58% rug) is weak, and n=62 on the low side.
- **Momentum:** high is slightly worse (rug 78% vs 68%), no real separation on doubling.
- **Social:** high is worse on doubling (4% vs 14%, n=51). Small n.
- **Survival:** high is slightly better (rug 60% vs 73%, n=89). Weak.
- **Claude PICK vs PASS:** pick median 24h is 0.04 and pass is 0.08. Pick rug rate is 76% vs 65% for pass, and picks doubled less (6% vs 15%, n=33 vs 301). Stage 3 is **not adding value**, and may be subtracting it.
- **Entry signal, 5-min spike over 20%:** yes (n=79) vs no (n=540). Down 40% at 1h is 53% vs 48%, up 50% at 1h is 11% vs 12%, median 24h is 0.09 vs 0.06. There is no meaningful separation, so I'm not recommending it.

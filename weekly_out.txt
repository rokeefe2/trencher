- **Stages:** picks rug at 70% and double at 21%. Passes rug at 59% and double at 15%. Picks have a *lower* median 24h than passes (0.02 vs 0.11), so that go-live check fails. This metric is distorted by post-exit moves and the feed quirk.
- **Components:**
  - **Holders** is strongly inverted (high rug 82% vs 48%, n≈910). Its weight is already at the 0.5 floor.
  - **Survival** is the best separator (high rug 53% vs 68%).

  - **Social** is mildly negative (doubled 8% vs 13%).
  - **Momentum** is mixed. High momentum doubles more (15% vs 10%) but rugs more (71% vs 57%).
